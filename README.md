# disc.is Companion

Connect your computer once. Use Soulseek and your local music from [disc.is](https://disc.is/).

[Download Companion 0.1.7 for Windows x64](https://github.com/1red-ryu/disc-is-companion/releases/download/companion-v0.1.7/disc-is-companion-0.1.7-windows-x64.zip) · [Release and checksums](https://github.com/1red-ryu/disc-is-companion/releases/tag/companion-v0.1.7)

## Setup

1. Extract the ZIP and open `disc.is-Companion.exe`.
2. Sign in to disc.is and open **Companion → Generate pairing code**.
3. Use **Open Companion & connect**, or copy the code and choose **Paste pairing code…** from the Companion tray menu.
4. Back on disc.is, choose a music folder and connect your Soulseek account. Select **Remember login** if you want automatic connection.

Search, browse other users' shared files, manage downloads, and control your rooms from disc.is. The app stays in the system tray. You do not need a room to search or listen privately. Your computer must remain awake and online.

Click an MP3 disc in Soulseek search for a temporary preview that plays while downloading. **+** saves a separate copy through your browser. Progressive preview currently supports MP3 without seeking; other supported formats can still be downloaded.

The website separately shows whether your computer is connected and whether Soulseek is signed in and sharing. Shared-folder selection, remembered login, reconnect, and optional startup with Windows are all available in the website's Companion menu.

## Updating

Quit the older Companion from its tray menu, extract the new ZIP, and launch the new executable. Your disc.is pairing and folder carry over. Older releases did not remember Soulseek passwords, so enter it once in the website and select Remember if desired.

## Privacy and source

Saved passwords use operating-system encryption on your computer and never return to the webpage. Remote controls use your authenticated disc.is account and a private encrypted connection. The app does not record the microphone or desktop audio.

The portable archive includes the Companion source, the compatible modified slskd runtime, its corresponding source, and licenses. Required audio tools are downloaded directly from a pinned, checksum-verified upstream release when needed; its archive and licenses remain in the local cache. This release repository contains no private website source, credentials, or user settings.
